---

## 📈 Key Findings

1. CAC is **increasing every quarter**, with Q4 being the highest.
2. The company's CAC (average **229**) is **far above** the industry target of **150**.
3. The widening gap indicates rising digital marketing inefficiency.
4. Q4 shows a steep jump, indicating aggressive but ineffective spend.

---

## 🧠 Business Implications

- The company is **overspending to acquire each new customer**.
- Marketing ROI is declining.
- If the trend continues, budget requirements will increase without growth.
- This threatens profitability and long-term customer LTV.

---

## ✅ Recommendation (MANDATORY)

To reach the CAC target of **150**, the company must **optimize digital marketing channels** by:

- Reducing low-ROI ad spend  
- Increasing automation in bidding  
- Improving audience segmentation  
- Enhancing retargeting and attribution accuracy  
- Strengthening organic acquisition funnels
